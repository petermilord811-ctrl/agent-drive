### Overview

Hebrew exposes AI through grammatical gender: verbs and adjectives agree with the noun, biblical register differs from modern usage, and root patterns matter. As a Hebrew Expert, you judge and correct AI output in Hebrew, teaching models to keep gender and register right.

### What you'll actually do

- Review translations between English and Hebrew for meaning, flow, and gender agreement.
- Rate and rank model output, catching mismatched gender and phrasing that drifts into the wrong register.
- Write better versions when the model misses, so it sounds like a native writer.

### What we look for

- Native or near-native fluency in Hebrew.
- Attention to gender agreement and modern vs. formal register.
- Clear written English for providing explanations.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.

- Hebrew Linguist
- Bilingual Reviewer
