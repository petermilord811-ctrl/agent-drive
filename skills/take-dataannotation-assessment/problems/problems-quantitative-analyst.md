## Bio

**Role:** Quantitative Analyst  
**Category:** Finance  
**Hiring cue:** Finance • 48 hired recently

### Overview

Models handle textbook finance fine and fall apart on the real thing: mispriced risk, subtle derivation errors, and market microstructure. As a Quantitative Analyst you'll stress-test AI financial reasoning and write the judgments a rigorous practitioner would actually stand behind.

### What you'll actually do

- Write prompts for pricing, risk, portfolio construction, and statistical modeling.
- Review AI output for flawed derivations and unsupported conclusions; trace the reasoning.
- Write correct rigor-based analyses when models fail.

### What we look for

- Experience in quantitative finance, trading, or risk background.
- Fluency in probability, statistics, and stochastic methods.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
