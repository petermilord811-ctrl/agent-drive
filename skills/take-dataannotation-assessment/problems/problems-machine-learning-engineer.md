### Overview

Models get stronger every month, but their advice about training, evaluation, data, and ML systems is often subtly wrong. As a Machine Learning Engineer you'll stress-test how models reason about ML systems — training dynamics, evaluation design, data pipelines — and write the answers a strong practitioner would give, so the next generation of models actually handles your field.

### What you'll actually do

- Write prompts that probe how models reason about training, debugging, evaluation, and productionizing ML systems.
- Review AI output for subtle mistakes: wrong loss formulations, and misdiagnosed training failures.
- Write the correct solution when the model falls short, grounded in real practitioner experience.

### What we look for

- Hands-on experience training, evaluating, or deploying models professionally or through serious personal work.
- Comfort with the modern ML stack; most tasks assume Python and PyTorch or JAX.
- Clear written English: your explanations are the training signal.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
