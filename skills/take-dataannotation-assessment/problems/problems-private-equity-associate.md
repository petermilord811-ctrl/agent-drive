### Overview

Models can build an LBO that looks clean and still skip the parts that matter. Someone has to check how private-market deals are underwritten — evaluating AI-built LBO models and diligence, return drivers, and downside cases.

### What you'll actually do

- Underwrite AI-built LBOs and the bridge that explains the IRR.
- Test the diligence: management projections, market, and the downside case the model conveniently skipped.
- Write investment-committee answers with the risks a partner would sign.

### What we look for

- Experience in private equity / growth equity.
- Fluency with LBO modeling.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
