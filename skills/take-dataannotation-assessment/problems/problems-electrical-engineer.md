## Bio

**Role:** Electrical Engineer  
**Category:** Engineering  
**Hiring cue:** Engineering • 32 hired recently

### Overview

DataAnnotation is looking for an experienced Electrical Engineer to evaluate how frontier AI models handle real electrical and electronics engineering work. Models can talk fluently about circuits; what they can't yet do reliably is the judgment built over years of designing, debugging, and shipping hardware. You'll teach AI to reason about circuits, systems, and physical constraints.

### What you'll actually do

- Design challenging engineering problems — circuits to specify, control loops to tune, power stages to size.
- Run problems through AI models and judge the output against a professional standard.
- Compare two model versions on identical prompts and say where each fell short.
- Critique model-generated derivations, schematics, component selections, and calculations.
- Flag concrete failures: wrong assumptions, sign/unit errors, unphysical results, invalid topologies, fabricated datasheet values.
- Problems draw on whatever you know best:
  - Analog, RF, and mixed-signal circuits.
  - Electromagnetics and antennas.
  - Control and systems.
  - Devices and materials.
