### Overview

We're looking for experienced bench scientists to help train AI models. You'll design realistic experimental-biology tasks, run them through AI agents, and grade the responses — the kind of work where you call a plate QC go/no-go, gate a flow panel, check a cloning strategy, or read a Western blot from raw images. Your judgment becomes the standard the models are measured against.

### What you'll actually do

- Design realistic experimental tasks from workflows you know: scenario, prompt, and supporting files (plate maps, reader output, gel/blot images, etc.).
- Run tasks through AI agents and grade deliverables (QC summaries, gating reports, cloning plans) against a professional standard.
- Write clear grading rubrics specifying what a correct deliverable must contain and why a response passes or fails.
- Flag concrete failures with evidence — ignored controls, wrong normalization, values that contradict, or fabricated values.
- Problems draw on whatever you know best:
  - Molecular and cell biology: cloning, cell culture, cell-based assays.
  - Protein biochemistry: purification, characterization, assay development.
  - Immunology and flow cytometry: panel design and gating.
  - Imaging and histology, microbiology and virology.
