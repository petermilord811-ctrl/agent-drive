## Bio

**Role:** Credit Analyst  
**Category:** Finance  
**Hiring cue:** Finance • 25 hired recently

### Overview

Models approve a borrower that any underwriter would flag: cash-flow coverage, leverage, and risk that look fine until you dig. Someone has to catch it — that someone is you — evaluating AI-built underwriting and writing how lending decisions are really made.

### What you'll actually do

- Underwrite AI credit analysis (cash-flow coverage, leverage, ability to service the debt).
- Test risk the model waves away (covenants, collateral, downside).
- Write credit decisions when the model fails — the reasoning a credit committee expects.

### What we look for

- Experience in credit analysis, underwriting, or credit risk.
- Fluency reading financial statements and assessing debt.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.

### Common backgrounds

- Credit Analyst
- Underwriter
- Credit Risk Manager
