## Bio

**Role:** Corporate Accountant  
**Category:** Accounting  
**Hiring cue:** Accounting • 30 hired recently

### Overview

Ask a model to close the books and it produces entries that foot perfectly and still miss eliminations, misapplied standards, or close mechanics. As a Corporate Accountant you'll evaluate AI on the general ledger, consolidations, and financial close against current standards.

### What you'll actually do

- Draft prompts probing GAAP treatment, accruals, and month-end close.
- Review AI output for missed eliminations, misapplied standards, and related errors.
- Write correct treatments when the model falls short.

### What we look for

- Hands-on corporate accounting or controllership experience.
- Comfort with GAAP.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.

### Common backgrounds

- Corporate Accountant
- General Ledger Accountant
- Staff Accountant
