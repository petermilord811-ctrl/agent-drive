**Category:** Languages

### Overview

Russian trips AI on structure it cannot fake: six cases, verb aspect, and agreement that must stay consistent. As a Russian Expert you judge and correct AI output so a native speaker notices.

### What you'll actually do

- Assess translations between English and Russian for accuracy, flow, and register.
- Rate and rank model output, flagging broken cases, wrong aspect, and misjudged formality.
- Rewrite text so it reads as native Russian.

### What we look for

- Native or near-native fluency in Russian.
- Firm grip on case, aspect, and the "ty" versus "vy" distinction.
- Clear written English for explanations.
- No degree required.

### Common backgrounds

- Russian Linguist
- Bilingual Reviewer
- Localization Specialist
