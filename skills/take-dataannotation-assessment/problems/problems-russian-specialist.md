### Overview

Russian trips AI on structure it cannot fake: six cases, verb aspect, and agreement that a native speaker notices. As a Russian Expert, you judge and correct AI output in Russian so models sound like people actually speak and write.

### What you'll actually do

- Assess translations between English and Russian for accuracy, flow, and register.
- Rate and rank model output in Russian, flagging broken cases, wrong aspect, and misjudged formality.
- Rewrite content so it reads as native Russian, not a gloss.

### What we look for

- Native or near-native fluency in Russian.
- A firm grip on case, aspect, and the ty versus vy distinction.
- Clear written English for providing training signals.
