## Bio

**Role:** Thai Specialist  
**Category:** Languages  
**Hiring cue:** Languages • 61 hired recently

### Overview

Thai is challenging for AI because words run together without spaces and tone/politeness particles change based on the speaker. A Thai Expert will judge and correct AI output in Thai to match everyday tone and politeness.

### What you'll actually do

- Review translations between English and Thai for meaning, flow, and politeness particles.
- Score and rank model output, catching wrong registers and unnatural word breaks.
- Write better versions when the model misses.

### What we look for

- Native or near-native fluency in Thai.
- A feel for register (plain vs. formal).
- Clear written English for explanations.
- No degree required.

### Common backgrounds

- Thai Linguist
- Bilingual Reviewer
- Localization Specialist
