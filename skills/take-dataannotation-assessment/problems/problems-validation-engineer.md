### Overview

We're looking for an experienced Validation Engineer to help train AI models. You'll write hard, verifiable engineering problems from your specialty, work out the answers yourself, and show us exactly where today's AI systems still fall short of a practicing engineer. This is a new and fast-growing area — teaching AI to reason like an engineer about sizing a component against a real load case, catching failure modes a spec sheet omitted, reading a CAD assembly, predicting the downstream effect of a design change, and applying engineering judgment.

### What you'll actually do

- Write challenging, closed-ended problems from your area of expertise requiring real engineering reasoning.
- Derive the correct answer, verify it, and write a worked solution.
- Test problems against AI systems and sharpen them until they challenge the model.
- Review AI-generated engineering analysis, designs, and technical documentation for correctness and real-world applicability.
- Review other contributors' problems and solutions for rigor.
- Problems draw on whatever you know best:
  - Mechanical design and CAD — geometry, clearances, tolerances, mechanisms, effect of design changes.
  - Structures and materials — stress, deflection, fatigue, buckling, material selection, failure analysis.
