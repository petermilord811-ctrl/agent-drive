### Overview

We're looking for an experienced Senior Mechanical Design Engineer to help train AI models. You'll write hard, verifiable engineering problems from your specialty — sizing a component against a real load case, catching failure modes not in the spec sheet, reading a CAD assembly, predicting downstream design-change impacts — and teach AI to reason like a practicing engineer.

### What you'll actually do

- Write challenging, closed-ended problems that require real engineering reasoning to get right.
- Derive and verify the correct answer before you put the problem in front of a model; write worked solutions.
- Test problems against AI systems and use the results to refine what is hard enough to challenge the model.
- Review AI-generated engineering analysis, designs, and technical documentation for correctness and real-world applicability.
- Review other contributors' work for correctness, ambiguity, and rigor.
- Problems draw on what you know best:
  - Mechanical design and CAD: geometry, clearances, tolerances, mechanisms, and the effect of design changes.
  - Structures and materials: stress, deflection, fatigue, buckling, material selection, and failure analysis.
