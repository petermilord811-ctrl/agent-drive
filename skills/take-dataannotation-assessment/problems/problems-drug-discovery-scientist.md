### Overview

We're looking for experienced drug discovery scientists to help train AI models. You'll design realistic discovery and preclinical tasks, run them through AI agents, and grade the responses — the kind of work where models talk fluently about SAR analysis, screening triage, DMPK and PK/PD interpretation, or candidate-selection calls and still get the science wrong. Your judgment becomes the benchmark those models are measured against.

### What you'll actually do

- Design realistic discovery tasks from workflows you know: scenario, prompt, and supporting files (assay result tables, SAR spreadsheets, DMPK summaries, study reports, program-review decks).
- Run tasks through frontier AI agents and grade deliverables (decision memos, screening workbooks, tox summaries).
- Write clear grading rubrics specifying what a correct deliverable must contain.
- Flag concrete failures with evidence — misread assay data, SAR conclusions unsupported by data, misinterpreted PK parameters, missed liabilities.
- Problems draw on whatever you know best:
  - Medicinal chemistry and SAR / lead optimization.
  - Computational chemistry and CADD.
  - Assay development and screening.
  - DMPK, PK/PD, ADME, and preclinical safety.
  - Translational and biomarker science, biologics and advanced modalities, and CMC / process development.
