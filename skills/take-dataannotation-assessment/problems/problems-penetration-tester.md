### Overview

Models can describe an exploit chain in confident detail and miss that step three never lands, the payload gets filtered, or the precondition they assumed doesn't exist. Someone has to judge what actually works. As a Penetration Tester you'll evaluate how models reason about offensive security — recon through lateral movement — expose where the chain breaks, and write the attack paths a real operator would use.

### What you'll actually do

- Test offensive reasoning across recon, exploitation, privilege escalation, and lateral movement.
- Find the broken chains: filtered payloads, impossible preconditions, and the hard part the model skipped.
- Write the attack path that actually works when the model falls short, grounded in real operator tradecraft.

### What we look for

- Hands-on experience in penetration testing, red-teaming, or CTF at a serious level.
- Fluency with attacker toolkits and how real exploit chains hang together.
- Clear written English: your explanations are the training signal.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
