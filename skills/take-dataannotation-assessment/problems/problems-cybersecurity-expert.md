### Overview

Models will hand you code that looks secure and quietly ships a hardcoded secret, a broken auth check, or an injection waiting for the right input. Someone has to catch that before it ships. As a Cybersecurity Expert you'll probe frontier models for unsafe code and exploit paths, rate their reasoning about real threats, and write the judgments that teach the next generation what secure actually means.

### What you'll actually do

- Probe models for weak spots across authentication, crypto, input handling, and secret management.
- Rate security reasoning against real-world practice — security theater, and the confidently wrong fix.
- Write the secure answer when the model falls short, with threat model and trade-offs spelled out.

### What we look for

- Professional experience in security engineering, defensive operations, or offensive work.
- Comfort reasoning about real threats: auth, crypto, injection, secret handling.
- Clear written English: your explanations are the training signal.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
