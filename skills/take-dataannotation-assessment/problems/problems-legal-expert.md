## Bio

**Role:** Legal Expert  
**Category:** Legal  
**Hiring cue:** Legal • 77 hired recently

### Overview

Ask a model a legal question and it answers with authority — then invents a citation that never existed. As a Legal Expert you'll grade AI legal reasoning against careful practitioner standards: contracts, statutes, and analysis that have to hold up when someone checks the sources.

### What you'll actually do

- Draft prompts probing contracts, statutes, and legal analysis.
- Review AI output for invented citations, misstated holdings, and related errors.
- Write correct analysis when the model falls short.

### What we look for

- JD or equivalent with practice experience.
- Comfort reading primary legal sources.
- Clear written English.
- We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.

### Common backgrounds

- Attorney
- Litigation Attorney
- Corporate Attorney

### Role sidebar (page 1)

- Up to $125+/hour
- Remote
- Flexible hours
- Independent contractor
- Weekly
