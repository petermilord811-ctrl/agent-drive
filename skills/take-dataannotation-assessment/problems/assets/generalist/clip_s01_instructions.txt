Let your abilities shine through. Your answers should be your own.

When completing this assessment, please do not use outside assistance. All of your responses should be written by you from scratch. No part of your answers should come from an AI assistant. Using these resources is grounds for removal from the platform. All research must be done using credible sources, and you must cite your sources where applicable.
