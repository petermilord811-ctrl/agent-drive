The reaction between 5-bromohex-2-ene and sodium methoxide in methanol proceeds
through an E2 (second-order elimination) mechanism.
In this case, sodium methoxide acts as the strong base, performing a concerted elimination
where it abstracts a β-hydrogen while bromide departs as a leaving group.
Thus,
C6H11Br + NaOMe → C6H10 + NaBr + CH3OH
The conjugated (E,E)-hexa-2,4-diene is the preferred product due to orbital overlap in the
conjugated π system. Na+ and Br- are present as spectator ions in solution.
