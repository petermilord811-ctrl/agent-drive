## Bio

**Role:** Radiologist  
**Category:** Medical  
**Hiring cue:** Medical • 18 hired recently

### Overview

We're looking for an experienced Radiologist to help train AI models. Measure how well models interpret medical images (chest x-rays, ultrasounds, nuclear medicine scans) — open to radiologists and imaging technologists.

### What you'll actually do

- Review de-identified studies alongside their clinical context.
- Write comprehensive, structured reports including subtle and incidental findings.
- Compare independent interpretations and log each discrepancy.
- Draw on radiography, ultrasound/sonography, nuclear medicine / molecular imaging, and cross-sectional imaging (CT / MRI).

### What we look for

- Radiology or medical imaging professional experience.
- Comfort with structured reporting and discrepancy logging.
- Clear written English.

### Common backgrounds

- Radiologist
- Imaging technologist
- Sonographer
