### Overview

Polish gives AI a lot to break: seven cases, verb aspect, and agreement that must stay consistent — plus formal pan/pani forms. As a Polish Expert, you judge and correct AI output in Polish so models learn the way people really speak it.

### What you'll actually do

- Assess translations between English and Polish for accuracy, flow, and register.
- Rate and rank model output in Polish, flagging broken cases, wrong aspect, and misjudged formality.
- Rewrite what falls short so it reads as native Polish.

### What we look for

- Native or near-native fluency in Polish.
- A firm grip on case, aspect, and the pan and pani forms.
- Clear written English for providing training signals.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
