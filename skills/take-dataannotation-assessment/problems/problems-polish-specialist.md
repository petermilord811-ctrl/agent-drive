### Overview

Polish gives AI a lot to break: seven cases, verb aspect, and agreement that must stay consistent. Native speakers correct AI so it sounds the way people really speak it.

### What you'll actually do

- Assess translations between English and Polish for accuracy, flow, and register.
- Rate and rank model output, flagging broken cases, wrong aspect, and misjudged formality.
- Rewrite text so it reads as native Polish rather than a translation gloss.

### What we look for

- Native or near-native fluency in Polish.
- Firm grip on grammar (case, aspect, pan/pani forms).
- Clear written English for providing training signals.
- No degree required.

### Common backgrounds

- Polish Linguist · Bilingual Reviewer · Localization Specialist
