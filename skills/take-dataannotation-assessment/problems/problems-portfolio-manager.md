**Category:** Finance

### Overview

Models can recommend a portfolio that reads well and quietly breaks every rule the book actually demands. Someone has to see the risk the model can't, and that someone is you — evaluating AI-built investment theses and writing the judgments that teach models how capital is actually put to work.

### What you'll actually do

- Evaluate AI investment theses on real names (catalyst, valuation).
- Interrogate construction the model waves away (position sizing, correlation, liquidity, risk).
- Write sharp calls when the model falls short of what a real book demands.

### What we look for

- Buy-side experience: portfolio management, hedge fund, or asset-management research.
- Fluency with portfolio construction, risk, and security analysis.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
