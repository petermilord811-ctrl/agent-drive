### Overview

Experienced Surgical Medicine Expert to train AI — complex surgical and perioperative problems, check reasoning, show where models fall short. New area focusing on OR workflow from indications to recovery.

### What you'll actually do

- Pose diverse, complex surgical and perioperative problems; evaluate answers.
- Rate model output for correctness, completeness, and safety against the current standard of care.
- Flag inaccuracies (missed contraindications to wrong operative steps).
- Write the correct answer with a clear rationale.

### What we look for

- Board certification in surgery with 3+ years post-residency, or current license to practice clinically in surgery (RN, NP, PA, or CRNA).
- Backgrounds: surgeons of all specialties, anesthesiologists, nurse anesthetists, perioperative/OR nurses, APCs in surgery.
- English fluency; clear writing that explains why output is wrong.
- Based in AU / CA / IE / NZ / UK / US.
