## Bio

**Role:** Management Consultant  
**Category:** Finance  
**Hiring cue:** Finance • 24 hired recently

### Overview

Models can draft a market-entry brief that looks partner-ready and still miss the real drivers. As a Management Consultant you'll test AI on business problems, judge outputs with a consultant's eye, and flag where framing, structure, or analysis fails — teaching models to think like a top-tier strategy advisor.

### What you'll actually do

- Write client-style briefs (market-entry, competitive landscape, etc.) and run them through AI models.
- Review AI responses like an associate's work before it reaches a partner — framing, structure, analytical rigor.
- Flag unsupported claims, faulty numbers, and weak recommendations.
- Compare responses head-to-head.

### What we look for / knowledge areas

- Strategy (market entry, growth).
- Market research (sizing, benchmarking).
- Leadership communications (board decks, steering-committee updates).
- Operations and transformation (process redesign).
- Clear written English.

### Common backgrounds

- Management Consultant
- Strategy Associate / Engagement Manager
- Corporate Strategy / BizOps

- Remote
