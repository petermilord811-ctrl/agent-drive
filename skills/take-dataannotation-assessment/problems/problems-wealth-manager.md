### Overview

Models give financial advice that sounds polished and still miss suitability, tax treatment, or withdrawal constraints. As a Wealth Manager you'll evaluate AI financial planning and write the judgments that teach models what sound personal-finance guidance looks like.

### What you'll actually do

- Evaluate AI financial plans (allocation, retirement, goals).
- Check tax treatment, suitability, estate / withdrawal logic.
- Write sound advice when the model fails.

### What we look for

- Experience in wealth management, financial advising, or planning.
- Familiarity with investments, tax, retirement, or estate planning.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
