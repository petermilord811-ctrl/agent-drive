### Overview

During an incident, models love a tidy story: one root cause, a clean timeline, a confident containment plan. Real incidents are messier — and the tidy story is often wrong. As an Incident Responder you'll grade how models handle detection, triage, and forensics, catch the mistakes that would blow a real response, and write the analysis a seasoned responder would trust.

### What you'll actually do

- Pressure-test response reasoning across detection, triage, forensics, and containment.
- Catch the mistakes that cost time: misread logs, missed persistence, broken chain of custody, containment that makes things worse.
- Write the correct playbook when the model falls short, grounded in how real incidents actually unfold.

### What we look for

- Hands-on experience in incident response, DFIR, or SOC operations.
- Comfort with logs, forensic artifacts, and timelines.
- Clear written English: your explanations are the training signal.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
