### Overview

Models sound reassuring and still get bedside care wrong: dosing detail no nurse would administer, triage that buries the sickest patient, discharge teaching that misses the one thing that keeps someone safe at home. As a Registered Nurse you'll evaluate AI reasoning on assessment, medication administration, and clinical protocols, and write the guidance an experienced nurse would give at the bedside.

### What you'll actually do

- Write prompts that probe nursing judgment: assessment, triage and prioritization, medication administration, and patient education.
- Review AI output for unsafe steps, wrong priorities, and protocol errors; rate the quality of the clinical reasoning.
- Write the correct answer when the model falls short, grounded in real bedside practice and current standards.

### What we look for

- Active or recent nursing experience as an RN or nurse practitioner.
- Comfort reasoning through assessment, medication safety, and care protocols.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required.
