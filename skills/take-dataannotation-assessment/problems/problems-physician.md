Medical questions are where model mistakes matter most — outdated guidance, missed contraindications. Evaluate AI clinical reasoning against current standards of care.

- Write prompts for differential diagnosis, treatment planning, and patient communication.

- Write correct answers grounded in current evidence / standards of care.
