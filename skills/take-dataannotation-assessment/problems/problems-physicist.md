### Overview

Models can recite physics and still get it wrong: dimensional errors, misapplied approximations, and derivations that skip the step where the physics actually happens. As a Physicist you'll evaluate AI reasoning across mechanics, E&M, quantum, and thermodynamics, and write solutions with the care of a good problem-set grader.

### What you'll actually do

- Craft problems that expose weak physical reasoning, from undergraduate mechanics to graduate-level theory.
- Review AI solutions for dimensional errors, misapplied approximations, and skipped steps, and rate the reasoning.
- Write the gold-standard solution when the model falls short, showing the physics clearly at every step.

### What we look for

- Graduate-level physics background or equivalent research experience.
- Comfort working problems across at least two core areas of physics.
- Clear written English: your explanations are the training signal.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
