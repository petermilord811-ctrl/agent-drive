## Bio

**Role:** Finance Expert  
**Category:** Finance  
**Hiring cue:** Finance • 67 hired recently

### Overview

Models can build a three-statement model that looks clean and still get the mechanics wrong. As a Finance Expert you'll evaluate AI-built financial analyses on real corporate scenarios, check the valuation assumptions and the accounting behind them, and write the judgments that teach the next generation of models what rigorous finance work looks like.

### What you'll actually do

- Stress-test AI-built models (three-statement linkages, DCF assumptions).
- Trace reasoning to expose plugged numbers / broken drivers when the model's own math doesn't support the conclusion.
- Write defensible analyses when models fall short.

### What we look for

- Professional experience in corporate finance, FP&A, or strategic finance.
- Fluency with modeling.
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.

### Common backgrounds

- FP&A Analyst
- Finance Business Partner
- Strategic Finance

### Role sidebar (page 1)

- Up to $125+/hour
- Remote
- Flexible hours
- Independent contractor
- Weekly
