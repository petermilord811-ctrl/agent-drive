```text
Greetings,
It is a pleasure to assist you with this request. Here are the instructions for preparing a London Fog.
London Fog
1. Steep one Earl Grey tea bag in half a cup of hot water.
2. Froth half a cup of milk of your choice.
3. Add one teaspoon of vanilla extract to the tea.
4. Pour the frothed milk over the tea.
5. Stir to combine all ingredients.
Flavor Variation: Lavender London Fog
For a floral twist, a Lavender London Fog incorporates a touch of lavender. Simply add a small amount of lavender simple syrup to the tea along with the vanilla extract before adding the milk. This creates a more fragrant and unique beverage, with the lavender simple syrup adding sweetness.
Vegan Alternative
A vegan London Fog is easily made by substituting dairy milk with a plant-based alternative such as oat milk, almond milk, or soy milk. Oat milk is particularly popular for its creamy texture, which closely mimics the feel of dairy milk when frothed.
```
