### Overview

Models can generate a slick-looking screen and completely miss the point: the flow that makes sense, the hierarchy that guides the eye, the contrast that keeps content readable. Good design still needs a human in the loop. As a Designer you’ll evaluate how models reason about UX and visual design, catch the choices that look fine and fail users, and write the critique that teaches the next generation what good design actually is.

### What you'll actually do

- Evaluate AI design work across user flows, layout, and visual hierarchy — whether it actually serves the user.
- Catch failures under the polish: clumsy flows, weak hierarchy, and accessibility problems a pretty mockup hides.
- Write stronger critiques when the model falls short — the kind of rationale a good reviewer would give.

### What we look for

- Professional experience in product, UX, or visual design, with a portfolio.
- Sharp eye for usability, hierarchy, typography, and accessibility.
- Clear written English for providing training signals.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.
