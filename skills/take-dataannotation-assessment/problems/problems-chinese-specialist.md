**Category:** Languages

### Overview

AI can string together grammatical Mandarin and still read as foreign: it fumbles measure words, slips into textbook phrasing, forces idioms (chengyu) where they don't belong, and blurs Simplified vs Traditional conventions. Native readers judge and correct that output so models sound like they were written in Chinese, not translated into it.

### What you'll actually do

- Review translations between English and Chinese for meaning, flow, and register — the way a careful editor would.
- Rate and rank model output, flagging clumsy phrasing or forced idioms.
- Rewrite content so it feels originally written in Chinese rather than something translated into it.

### What we look for

- Native or near-native fluency in Chinese (Mandarin).
- Comfort with Simplified and Traditional text.
- Clear written English for providing explanations.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.

- Chinese Linguist
- Bilingual Reviewer
- Localization Specialist
