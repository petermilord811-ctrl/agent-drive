**Category:** Finance  
**Hiring cue:** Finance • 29 hired recently

### Overview

Models write a pitch deck's worth of confident deal analysis and miss what a deal team lives on. Someone has to catch it before it reaches a client — evaluating AI-generated deal analysis, checking valuation and transaction mechanics, and writing the judgment a banker defends a number with.

### What you'll actually do

- Review AI deal analysis across M&A and financings: comparable companies, precedent transactions, and DCF.
- Pressure-test mechanics (accretion/dilution, sources and uses, and related).
- Write banker-grade answers with valuation ranges you could defend in a live process.

### What we look for

- Professional experience in IB, M&A, or transaction advisory.
- Fluency with valuation (comps, precedents, DCF, accretion/dilution).
- Clear written English.
- No degree required. We care about what you can do, not where you learned it.

- M&A Associate
- Transaction Advisory
